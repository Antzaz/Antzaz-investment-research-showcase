How to read this tab

Expected return is a base-case annualized scenario estimate and should be considered together with conviction, business quality, valuation and downside risk. The thesis is separated into business case, valuation, catalysts, risks and explicit falsification conditions.

EQUITY RESEARCH PROCESS

From filings to valuation and thesis monitoring

The production research engine combines issuer/regulatory source checks, historical financial analysis, segment economics, peer benchmarking, scenario valuation, risk controls and thesis monitoring. Public company case studies can be added here without exposing private workbooks.

1. Fundamentals

Revenue, margins, FCF, ROIC/ROE, leverage, capital allocation and segment trends.

2. Valuation

DCF, scenario analysis, trading multiples, reverse-DCF expectations and sensitivity testing.

3. Thesis control

Catalysts, key risks, falsification conditions, KPI monitoring and periodic review.

What the full model supports

SEC and issuer filings, company IR materials, financial statement history, segment analysis, dynamic peer selection, ownership analysis, news-impact review, DCF/scenario valuation, stress testing, model-quality controls, Excel outputs and portfolio integration.
